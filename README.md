The analysis provides insights into supermarket sales report of a business over a one-year period(2024). The report covers three different branches of the business in terms of  customer types, gender, product line, unit price, quantity, payment and ratings.

# AIMS AND OBJECTIVES
## AIM
The main aim of this analysis is to understand the overall performance of the business, the contribution of the various branches as well as the gap with a view to offer recommendation on areas of potential improvement.
## OBJECTIVE
To assess the total annual revenue generated 
To determine the rating of business by customers
To analyze the performance of three branches and the gender spread
To assess the revenue generation of customer types and determine the contribution of various payment channels
To evaluate the monthly and quarterly sales performance 
Compare the revenue generated by different product lines
