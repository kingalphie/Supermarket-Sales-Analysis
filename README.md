# Supermarket-Sales-Analysis
The analysis provides insights into supermarket sales report of a business over a one-year period(2024). The report covers three different branches of the business in terms of  customer types, gender, product line, unit price, quantity, payment and ratings.

# AIMS AND OBJECTIVES
## AIM
The main aim of this analysis is to understand the overall performance of the business, the contribution of the various branches as well as the gap with a view to offer recommendation on areas of potential improvement.

## OBJECTIVES
-To assess the total annual revenue generated 
-To determine the rating of business by customers
-To analyze the performance of three branches and the gender spread
-To assess the revenue generation of customer types and determine the contribution of various payment channels
-To evaluate the monthly and quarterly sales performance 
=Compare the revenue generated by different product lines

 # CONCLUSIONS AND RECOMMENDATIONS

 ### CONCLUSION
The Supermarket sales data analyzed shows insightful information on areas of strength as well as areas of improvement.
Highest sales was seen in April($132,083) and lowest in August($19,440) while the overall rating was 34,790 . 

 ### RCOMMENDATION
Protect and defend the major revenue drivers like Home & Lifestyle, Health & Beauty, and Sports & Travel product lines.
Ensure constant availability of Home & Lifestyle, and Health & Beauty product lines to drive sales
Targeted product campaign around Electronics, Food & Beverage, and Fashion & Accessories Products
Drive engagement and campaign on Fashion and Accessories while maintaining focus on the other products
Consider discount offer on Fashion and Accessories to stimulate customers interest e.g buy 5 items get 1 free. Targeting female clients.
